    Privacy Policy

    Effective Date: November 2026

    CareerMatch AI is engineered on the principle of data minimization and transparent scoring. We believe student career data belongs strictly to the individual.

    1. Transient Session Privacy

    When you select the "Do not save my resume on the server" option, parsing occurs exclusively in transient memory. No text, contact details, or PDF bytes are recorded into our persistent database.

    2. Zero Model Training on Student Resumes

    We never sell your resume to third-party recruiters or use candidate submissions to train external generative models.

    3. Scoped Local Storage

    Guest sessions store all data under scoped browser keys (IndexedDB cm:v1:*). You have full control to export or wipe this data at any time via Settings.

    Terms of Service

    Effective Date: November 2026

    CareerMatch AI provides algorithmic benchmarks and career planning intelligence for educational and preparation purposes.

    All placement readiness scores and salary benchmarks are calculated from public job feeds and curated Indian tech role matrices. Scores represent advisory guidelines and do not constitute an explicit job guarantee.

    Cookie & Storage Policy

    Zero Third-Party Tracking

    CareerMatch AI does not use third-party analytics cookies, cross-site trackers, or advertising beacons.

    We strictly utilize browser localStorage and IndexedDB for essential application state: your theme preference, active authentication JWT, and locally cached career roadmaps.
