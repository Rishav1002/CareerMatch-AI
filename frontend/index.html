    Privacy Policy & Data Charter

    Version 4.0.0 · Effective Date: November 2026
    Manage My Data
    Plain Language Summary

    CareerMatch AI is engineered on strict data minimization for Indian engineering students and early-career developers. We process your resume and assessment responses solely to benchmark technical readiness and provide learning recommendations. When you check "Do not save my resume", processing is strictly in transient memory. Guest sessions expire after 30 days. We never sell candidate data to recruitment brokers or third parties, and we never train external generative AI foundation models on your submitted resumes. You can export or permanently erase your data at any time from your Settings.

    1. Data Fiduciary & Regulatory Framework

    This Privacy Policy is published in compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act 2023), the Information Technology Act, 2000, and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011. It also upholds data protection standards for international visitors under the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

    Data Fiduciary Identity: CareerMatch AI Technologies [PLACEHOLDER: Legal Entity Name, Tech Innovation Park, Sector 62, Noida, UP 201309, India. Contact: privacy@careermatch.ai].

    2. Data We Collect & Purpose Specification

    • Contact & Account Identifiers: Name, email address, and encrypted credentials (salted bcrypt hash) when registering an account.
    • Career & Educational Documents: Resume text, uploaded PDF/DOCX files, technical skills, projects, degrees, certifications, and work experience.
    • Interactive Evaluation Artifacts: Mock interview answer transcripts, speech durations, AI scoring feedback, and coding assessment quiz responses.
    • Public Technical Proofs: Public GitHub repository statistics, commit frequency, and primary language usage (fetched only upon your explicit request).
    • Essential Technical Telemetry: Session identifier cookie (cm_sid) and temporary IP logs utilized solely for rate-limiting and DDoS mitigation.

    3. Lawful Basis & Explicit Consent

    Under Section 6 of the DPDP Act 2023, personal data is processed strictly on the basis of your free, specific, informed, and unambiguous consent. You signal consent by actively uploading a resume, registering an account, or starting a mock interview. You maintain the right to withdraw consent and delete your records at any time.

    4. Resume Processing & Google Gemini AI Boundaries

    To deliver automated skill extraction and role gap benchmarks, extracted text excerpts are evaluated using the Google Gemini API. The following strict boundaries govern this processing:

    • No Foundation Model Training: Under enterprise API service terms, student resume submissions are not used to train or refine Google Gemini models.
    • Data Isolation: AI requests are processed in private, encrypted memory channels and are discarded once analysis is returned.
    • Ephemeral Privacy Mode: Checking "Do not save my resume on the server" executes analysis in RAM only without writing to the database.

    5. Data Retention & Automated Cleanup

    • Guest User Sessions: Unauthenticated analyses linked to temporary session cookies (cm_sid) are automatically purged after 30 days.
    • Registered Student Accounts: Data remains stored until you trigger the "Delete my account and data" command or request administrative erasure.

    6. Student Rights under DPDP Act 2023

    You have full, unhindered agency over your personal data:

    • Right to Access & Portability: Obtain a structured JSON copy of all stored records via Settings → Export JSON (calls /api/profile/export).
    • Right to Correction: Update your student profile, degree, and verified skill roster at any time.
    • Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten): Permanently delete all resumes, analysis runs, interview transcripts, and accounts via Settings → Purge Data (calls /api/profile/delete).
    • Right to Grievance Redressal: Submit grievances directly to our appointed Grievance Officer.
    • Right to Nominate: Nominate another individual to exercise data rights in the event of death or incapacity.

    7. Protection of Minors (DPDP Act Section 9)

    CareerMatch AI is designed for college students and adults aged 18 and older. Users under 18 years of age must obtain verifiable parental or legal guardian consent before using the platform. We do not track, profile, or deliver targeted advertisements to minors.

    8. Sub-processors & Cross-Border Transfers

    We work with trusted cloud infrastructure providers bound by strict data protection agreements:

    • Render Services Inc. (USA/EU): Containerized web hosting and API routing under TLS 1.3 encryption.
    • Neon Tech Inc. (AWS us-east): Serverless PostgreSQL relational database storage with encrypted storage volumes.
    • Google LLC (USA): Gemini API semantic intelligence processing with zero persistent training retention.
    • GitHub Inc. (USA): Public developer API consumption for portfolio verification.

    9. Technical Security Safeguards

    Security measures include end-to-end TLS 1.3 transport encryption, salted bcrypt password hashes, HttpOnly cryptographically signed SameSite=Lax session cookies, strict owner-scoped query filtering, and automated rate limiting on costly API endpoints.
