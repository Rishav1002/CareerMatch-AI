📐 Scoring Mathematics & Formula Transparency +

Unlike generic LLM wrappers that output random scores, CareerMatch AI calculates weighted indices using strictly transparent formulas:

Overall Match Score = (Mandatory Match Pct × 0.50) + (Preferred Match Pct × 0.30) + (Project Evidence Pct × 0.20)

Every matched skill requires a verifiable text anchor from your resume. If a skill does not exist in your resume, it receives exactly 0 points until verified.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is CareerMatch AI different from generic resume score checkers? +
Most online checkers generate superficial keyword percentages without context. CareerMatch AI verifies mandatory vs. preferred requirements, locates sentence-level evidence quotes, checks GitHub commit history, and generates a personalized learning roadmap with curated courses.
Is this platform tailored to the Indian tech hiring ecosystem? +
Yes. The salary benchmarks, role matrices (TCS Digital, Razorpay, Flipkart, Swiggy, tier-1 tech GCCs), and ATS evaluation criteria are calibrated specifically for Indian engineering colleges and lateral hiring tracks in Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, Gurugram, and Noida.
Can I export my career report as a clean PDF? +
Yes. CareerMatch AI features a bespoke field-guide print stylesheet. Pressing Print or "Export PDF" strips away all navigation and formats your career report as an editorial ledger suitable for mentors or placement coordinators.
